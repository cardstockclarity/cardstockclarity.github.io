Offerings

All sessions are reflective and exploratory in nature and may induce intense emotions. Sessions close with a healing/uplifiting oracle card reading to help restore balance. The core aim of my sessions is to transform difficult emotions and behaviors into learnings fueling your growth.


Standard Tarot Reading

15+ minutes card reading for self-reflection or guidance on topics such as career, relationships, life direction, personal growth, etc. I use tarot spreads to improve clarity and structure to the reading’s flow. Please note readings may describe a potential future, depending on certain conditions changing or remaining the same; but it represents just one of many possible outcomes shaped by your choices and free will. Overall, my readings are reflective—not predictive—and aim to empower your decision-making.

Archetypes Mapping

45+ min exploring your archetypes, which are the inner patterns we embody — our motivations, instincts, protective strategies and more that we naturally take on in relationships, work, etc. These patterns often operate beneath the surface, shaping our choices, strengths, and challenges. Together we'll map your personal archetypes to a 'Chart of Origin' wheel, designed to help you understand your core patterns and the inner forces influencing your current chapter of life. Prerequisite questionnaire required.

In-Depth Shadow Work

Available by invitation since this is a more personalized session based on my ongoing knowledge of your challenges. This focuses on the exploration of the emotions or memories hidden in the 'shadows' of your psyche. By bringing these aspects into awareness with compassion, you create space for healing, integration, and renewed clarity. There is no fixed duration as reflective pauses are encouraged as needed, allowing the process to unfold at your natural pace. This may surface past wounds or repressed traumas.


Clinical-Style Exploration

60+ minutes reflective session using evidence-informed psychology cards only. This offering excludes all spiritual, metaphysical, or esoteric tools and language, designed specifically for those who prefer a non-mystical approach. These sessions use imagery, prompts, emotions, and real-world themes to help you explore what you're feeling, clarify your thoughts, and identify patterns that may be blocking your progress. It’s a supportive, insight-driven option for anyone seeking clarity and understanding without spiritual symbolism.

Metaphoric Associations

60+ minutes session that exclude the use of any tarot cards. This may explore themes that require a sense of trust and vulnerability so I do not recommend this for our first meeting. This begins with a brief meditation to help bring the subconscious mind closer to the surface. From there, we explore images with no predefined meanings to invite your inner self to express its story, revealing underlying emotions and themes. Together, we uncover what’s seeking expression and close by tuning into your intuition for guidance.

Dream Interpretation

60+ minutes guided session designed for those who were able to recall and record details from their dreams. In this session, we explore the symbolic language of your subconscious by unpacking the dream, and tracing emotional themes that may be seeking your attention. We will look at the patterns, symbols, and archetypes within your dream and connect them to your waking life for insight, self-awareness, and practical guidance. Aims to help illuminate hidden tensions, unmet needs, or discover new directions emerging from within. Prerequisite questionnaire required.


Creativity Brainstorm

60+ min guided exploration (using creativity and problem solving cards/spreads) to help you spark new ideas for projects, business, products or anything else you’re trying to create. With my background in software consulting, web design, and other creative endeavors, I can offer practical insights drawn from my own experience as well. This is part intuitive and part collaborative brainstorming to help you get unstuck and think in new directions. I act as a creative thinking partner who helps you articulate ideas, refine concepts, and see possibilities from angles you may not have considered.

Financial Foundations

60+ min discussion incorporating abundance-focused prompts to attract a more empowered relationship with money. With a bachelor’s in Finance and a master’s in Economics, I can help you understand the fundamentals of building financial stability and setting yourself up for long-term success. Whether you’re just getting started or wanting to improve your relationship with money, I offer help in uncovering the emotional patterns behind your spending and provide guidance on budgeting, saving strategies, and mindset shifts that support healthier financial habits.

Couples Card Session

This session uses relationship based cards to potentially (a) explore relational dynamics, recurring patterns, and emotional undercurrents, (b) identify core needs each partner can focus on to strengthen alignment and mutual support or (c) support in navigating a specific conflict, gently guiding both partners through with structure and intention. This is not traditional couples therapy, but a facilitated exploratory experience that helps both partners feel seen, heard, and understood. Suitable for romantic partners at any stage, from dating to long-term commitment.

Colored Emotions (Meditation)

Reflective session where we use emotion-themed cards as a roadmap for an gentle, customized guided meditation based on the relationship or life event you are processing. Each card represents a single emotion—whether joy, fear, gratitude, or uncertainty. Together, we’ll acknowledge, give thanks to, and gently release each emotion. This session is built to aid you in achieving a renewed sense of emotional balance and clarity. This is a perfect offering for anyone wanting to honor their emotional landscape in a mindful, guided journey. Prerequisite questionnaire required.

Immersive Card Readings

90+ min personalized experience only available by invite to those whom completed multiple sessions. It combines gamified card readings with music, customized AI-generated imagery and immersive storytelling to hopefully wrap-up the sessions. It’s designed to help you re-experience your own story from a new perspective, for an engaging, therapeutic, and healing experience. Inspired from different chapters of my life — from my many years working in the AI and technology space to the imaginative worlds I escaped into through role-playing video games as a child.

RATES: For a limited time, any session from the first column of offerings (standard tarot reading, clinical-style exploration, creativity brainstorm and couples card sessions) is free. Tips are entirely optional, but greatly appreciated.


SENSITIVE CONTENT WARNING: By default, I exclude using 'darker' decks with more intense symbolic or literal depictions of nudity, sexuality, violence, or mental health struggles such as self-harm. If requested in advance, I can also make sure to avoid using decks with any level of such imagery.

However for shadow work, more intense or unsettling imagery may be helpful in reflecting those hidden aspects of ourselves we try to avoid. Such images can help bring these feelings or memories to the surface to be seen, acknowledge, and processed so that they lose some of their power leading to more wholeness and self-acceptance. It's not the scary image itself that heals, but the process of working through what it evokes. If you’re open to this approach, you can let me know you would like to consider one of my 'darker' decks for readings.

Conduct Code: Sessions are meant to be supportive and respectful. I may end service if the interaction feels inappropriate or misaligned with my values.



DISCLAIMER: I like to use card readings as tools for self-awareness, introspection, and healing support. They may complement personal growth, but they are not a replacement for therapy or professional mental health care.
