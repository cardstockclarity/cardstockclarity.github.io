Supporting Personal Growth & Healing

...through card readings.

Hi 😊. I am LB and I'm a mindfulness practitioner who leverages introspective card readings and art therapy to guide discussions supporting your personal growth and mental healing. Each session is a dialogue—an invitation to slow down, reflect and reconnect with the deeper layers of your own story.

I use artistically expressive psychology, tarot and oracle cards as mindfulness tools for self-reflection rather than fortune-telling. These visual and symbolic systems help uncover patterns, emotions, and inner truths that may be influencing your path. Through this form of spiritual psychotherapy (metaphysical tools combined with talk therapy), my aim is to support you in the current phase of life you are in, as well as the transitions you’re moving through.

I believe everyone is inherently intuitive, and these sessions may help you access and trust your intuition. I also believe that the universe sends us messages to prepare and guide us through life’s events. These messages can appear in countless ways—such as dreams, déjà vu, synchronicities, repeating numbers, song lyrics, symbols, or chance encounters—but tarot offers a more direct and tangible way to receive such communication. Many may read tarot and oracle cards differently, but for me, the cards are just a tool that helps the universe and our intuition work together to bring forward the messages we need to hear. That is the extent of divination in my practice.

During card readings, typically I will ask questions for further reflection or to solidify the message as I share my interpretations. I will always encourage you to share your thoughts; whether you agree, disagree, or see something different. Therefore, it is more of a collaborative approach—a two-way, open discussion that aims to be both therapeutic and insightful. My sessions focus on personal empowerment, helping you uncover meaning and clarity for your next steps.

About The Cards:

Tarot
  • I use the tarot system because it is a rich and structured framework that is able to cover the entire spectrum of the human experience within 78 cards. This makes tarot a powerful tool for self-reflection, allowing us to explore your inner landscape from different perspectives.
  • I also have a collection of tarot decks spanning different artistic styles. I will try to use a deck that most aligns with your preferencse since connecting with the art helps the message resonate on a more personal level.
Oracle
  • Unlike tarot, which follows a structured system, oracle decks are open-ended with each one carrying its own language, theme, and energy.
  • I include oracle card readings in every session to help clarify or expand the messages that surface, offering gentle affirmation and closure at the end of our reading.
Psychology
  • Psychology-based cards are therapeutic tools created by psychologists and counselors to support emotional awareness, inner dialogue, and personal growth. They are often inspired by approaches like Schema Therapy, Internal Family Systems (IFS), Metaphoric Associative Therapy, etc. which help visualize and externalize inner parts, emotions, and life themes.
  • I incorporate these cards into sessions because they bridge intuitive and evidence-based perspectives.
Other
  • I-Ching Cards - card adaptation of the ancient Chinese divination system, that is used to understand the flow of change in your life with philosophical depth.
  • Lenormand Cards - for clear and situational readings that focus on the who, what, when, and how of real-life events
  • Affirmations Cards - designed to shift your mindset, boost self-esteem, and give positive reinforcement
  • Playing Cards - reading an ordinary deck to gain quick, practical insights into life situations and energetic dynamics
  • Other items I feel a pull towards may be a potential conduit for intuitive reading, so at times I may incorporate other unorthodox tools.
